GOONO

The crew’s card game. Match the colour or number. First to get rid of their cards wins.

You play as yourself, with your driver avatar.

Play together

One phone hosts and gets a code. Everyone else types the code in. Phones can be on Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Play the crew

No signal? Play against computer players from the crew.

How to play

Play a card that matches the top card’s colour or number. Can’t go? Pick one up. Tea Break skips the next player, Wrong Way Van reverses, Snag List makes the next player pick up 2. Goono cards change the colour, and Mum’s Calling changes it and makes the next player pick up 4. Down to your last card? Tap GOONO! before someone catches you.
